Mango + Strawberry Chia Bowl

I love this for breakfast!

Ingredients

    • 4 Strawberries (sliced vertically)

    • 1 Small Alphonso Mango (cubed)

    • 4 Tablespoons of Greek Yogurt

    • 2 Tablespoons of Chia seeds (pre-soaked)

Preparation

Preparing the Yogurt scoops

    • Use an ice cream scoop (or the equivalent) to scoop two scoops of greek yogurt.

    • Aim to use a thick yogurt base for ease of scooping

    • Refrigerate for 15-30 minutes to make sure they hold their shape.

Preparing the Fruit

    • Wash your strawberries and mango

    • Slice the strawberries vertically and put them aside.

    • Cube the mango and set aside.

      • If you are using a big mango slice one of the bigger cheeks of the mango.

    • Refrigerate for 10 minutes to weaken color bleed into the yogurt but that is optional

Preparing the Chia Seeds

    • In some water, add your chia seeds.

    • NEVER EVER EVER EAT UNSOAKED CHIA SEEDS AS IT CAN LEAD TO SERIOUS HEALTH ISSUES

Instructions

  • Spread your chia seed paste on the bottom base of the bowl.

  • Place two yogurt balls and place them to the right

  • Put the mangos on the upper quadrant of the left in the bowl

  • Put the strawberries on the lower quadrant of the left in the bowl
